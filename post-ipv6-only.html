    Living on IPv6-Only
  • PostedAug 30, 2026
  • Reading time3 min

  • For three months, one VLAN at home had no IPv4. These are my notes on what that was like.

    The setup

    The firewall ran NAT64 and DNS64, so IPv6-only clients could still reach IPv4-only sites through translation.

    What worked

    Browsers, streaming, phones, laptops, and most apps. It was boring, which is exactly the point of a good transition.

    What broke

    Game consoles, an older printer, a few VPN clients, and anything that hard-codes an IPv4 address. Each had a workaround, but only after some digging.

    I'd do it again. The details are on the IPv6-Only Test Network project page.
