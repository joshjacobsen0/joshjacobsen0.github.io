    VLANs for Humans
  • PostedOct 6, 2026
  • Reading time3 min

  • A friend asked me how to start segmenting their home network. Here is the short version I gave them.

    Start with three

    Trusted, IoT, and Guest is enough. Add more only when you have a reason.

    Default deny

    Let each VLAN reach the internet and nothing else. Then open specific holes one at a time, such as letting Trusted reach the printer, and write down why.

    Mind the mDNS

    AirPlay, printers, and casting all rely on multicast discovery, which stops at the VLAN boundary. An mDNS reflector on the firewall fixes it without opening everything up.
