    Downgrading an iPhone 5 to iOS 6
  • PostedSep 28, 2026
  • Reading time3 min

  • Getting an iPhone 5 back to iOS 6 is possible, but it isn't one click. Here's what worked for me.

    Before you start

    Check that the device's SHSH blobs were saved for iOS 6, since Apple no longer signs it. Without them the downgrade isn't possible. Back up everything first.

    The process

    Put the device in DFU mode, restore with a custom firmware image, and boot with a tethered helper from a computer. Expect a few tries.

    Living with it

    Tethered means a reboot needs a computer to boot again, so I keep the phone charged and rarely restart it. It's a bench device, not my daily driver.

    Everything here applies to my own hardware. Only do this to devices you own.
