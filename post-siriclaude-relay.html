  • SiriClaude started as a joke: what if the old Siri on an iPhone 5 could hand a question to something smarter? Now it's the project I open most often.

    The problem

    iOS 6 speaks an old version of TLS that modern API servers refuse. The phone literally can't complete the handshake.

    The workaround

    A small relay on my network accepts a plain request from the phone, makes the modern HTTPS call, and streams the answer back. The tweak on the phone only needs to know the relay's address.

    What I learned

    Constraints are fun. With a few hundred megabytes of RAM and no modern libraries, every feature has to justify itself.
