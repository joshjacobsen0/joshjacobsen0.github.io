Overview

  • A tiny battery-powered capture box: two Ethernet ports, a passive tap, and a web page you can open from a phone to start, stop, and download captures.

    Goal

    Walk up to a misbehaving port, plug the box in line, and have a .pcap on my phone in under a minute. No laptop, no mirror-port config.

    Open problems

    Keeping up with 1 Gbps line rate on a Pi, and fitting everything in a case that doesn't look like a science fair project.

Parts

  • Board Raspberry Pi 4
  • NICs 2x USB 3 gigabit
  • Power USB-C battery pack
  • Software tcpdump, Flask
